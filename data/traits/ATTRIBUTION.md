# Vendored trait data — attribution

The four `*_extract.json` / `*_eval.json` files in this directory are copied from
the PERSONA project (Feng et al.), **MIT licensed, © 2025 Xiachong Feng et al.**

Source: `data_generation/trait_data_extract/` and `data_generation/trait_data_eval/`.
Renamed here to make the split explicit; contents are unmodified.

OCEAN axis mapping (from PERSONA's README):

| Axis | High pole | Low pole |
|---|---|---|
| Openness | `inventive` | **`consistent`** |
| Extraversion | **`outgoing`** | `solitary` |

`consistent` (our primary trait) is the LOW pole of **Openness** — traditionalism,
preferring proven methods. It is NOT a Conscientiousness/reliability trait.
`outgoing` (our out-of-domain bleed trait) is Extraversion — a different axis.
